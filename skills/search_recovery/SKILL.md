# Search recovery
1. Identify the most important unresolved clue from the evidence ledger.
2. Use up to three distinct search actions when each addresses a different unresolved clue. Open promising results as they appear; if earlier results do not appear relevant, a third targeted search is allowed before opening candidates. Once the search budget is exhausted, stop searching and inspect the strongest candidates.
3. Reformulate around that missing clue with technical synonyms, aliases, dates stated in the question, verified full names, or a distinctive relation; do not invent details or repeat the same query. Use additional independent clue queries within one search action when it helps cover separate paths.
4. Split compound questions into independent clue searches and compare candidate names and document evidence.
5. Verify candidate clues in opened documents with exact-phrase lookup or a targeted candidate-plus-clue search.
6. Use up to three distinct searches only when each adds new documents or checks a different unresolved clue. Abstain only after checking promising current candidates.
